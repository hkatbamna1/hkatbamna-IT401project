We connect people with the live music happening around them.

Concert discovery can feel fragmented. The artists you follow are in one app, venue calendars are in another, and the perfect small show can slip by unnoticed.

Encore brings nearby concerts together and turns them into recommendations shaped by your location and music taste. Whether you want a high-energy Friday night or an intimate set from someone new, we help you find a show worth remembering.
